# SEARCH FOR MR. AND MS. IDSC 2026
### IDSC 18TH FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY CELEBRATION
#### OFFICIAL PAGEANT MECHANICS AND GUIDELINES

---

## I. GENERAL DESCRIPTION

**Mr. and Ms. IDSC 2026** is the official search for the male and female students who best embody the intelligence, character, leadership, and pride of IDSC, held as part of the grand celebration of the 18th Founding Anniversary of the institution.

This pageant is not merely a display of beauty, but a holistic celebration of personality, intelligence, character, talent, and advocacy. It aims to promote camaraderie, excellence, and healthy competition among all departments of IDSC as the institution marks eighteen (18) remarkable years of service and excellence in education.

The competition serves as a platform for recognition and representation, honoring candidates who embody grace, intelligence, leadership, confidence, and pride while representing their respective departments with excellence and integrity, in celebration of IDSC's rich history and continuing legacy.

---

## II. REPRESENTATION AND CANDIDACY

- **Number of Representatives:** Each department must officially send TWO (2) representatives; one (1) male and one (1) female; who will compete separately as individual candidates for Mr. and Ms. IDSC 2026.
- **Department Representation:** Each candidate shall represent their respective department throughout the competition.
- **Submission Deadline:** Official names of the two representatives must be submitted on or before August 26, 2026 to the organizing committee.
- **Endorsement & Validation:** Only candidates officially endorsed by their department and validated by the organizers shall be allowed to compete.

---

## III. QUALIFICATIONS OF CANDIDATES

All candidates must meet the following requirements:

1. **Enrollment Status:** Must be officially enrolled for the First Semester of Academic Year 2026–2027 at IDSC in any academic program.
2. **Registration:** Must be an officially registered representative of their department for the IDSC 18th Founding Anniversary Celebration.
3. **Good Standing & Character:** Must be a bona fide student of the department they are representing, of good moral character, and possesses the poise and confidence to represent IDSC with pride and dignity.
4. **Academic & Disciplinary Standing:** Must be in good academic and disciplinary standing as certified by their respective department.
5. **Attendance:** Must attend all scheduled rehearsals, meetings, and official activities set by the organizers.
   - *Note on Absence:* Failure to attend rehearsals without a valid written justification approved by the organizing committee may result in point deductions, loss of eligibility for special awards, or disqualification.
6. **Code of Conduct:** Must conduct themselves with professionalism, respect, and good decorum at all times before, during, and after the pageant.
7. **Previous Titleholders:** Must not have been previously crowned Mr. or Ms. IDSC in any prior staging of the pageant.

> *Only candidates who fully comply with these qualifications shall be allowed to compete.*
