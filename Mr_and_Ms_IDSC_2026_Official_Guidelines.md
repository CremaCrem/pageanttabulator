# SEARCH FOR MR. AND MS. IDSC 2026
## IDSC 18TH FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY CELEBRATION
### OFFICIAL PAGEANT MECHANICS AND GUIDELINES

## I. GENERAL DESCRIPTION
The Search for Mr. and Ms. IDSC 2026 is the official pageant of Infotech Development Systems Colleges, Inc. (IDSC), conducted as one of the major highlights of the institution’s 18th Founding Anniversary Celebration.
The pageant is more than a celebration of physical appearance. It is a holistic competition that recognizes personality, intelligence, character, leadership, confidence, advocacy, and institutional pride.
It aims to promote camaraderie, excellence, sportsmanship, and healthy competition among the different departments of IDSC while providing students with an opportunity to represent their respective departments with dignity and distinction.
Ultimately, the Search for Mr. and Ms. IDSC 2026 seeks to crown student ambassadors who best embody the values, aspirations, and continuing legacy of IDSC.

## II. REPRESENTATION AND CANDIDACY
Each department shall officially send two (2) representatives:
* One (1) male candidate for Mr. IDSC 2026; and
* One (1) female candidate for Ms. IDSC 2026.

Male and female candidates shall compete separately in their respective categories. Each candidate shall officially represent their respective department throughout the competition.

The official names of the two (2) representatives must be submitted to the Organizing Committee on or before August 26, 2026. Only candidates officially endorsed by their respective departments and duly validated by the Organizing Committee shall be allowed to compete.

## III. QUALIFICATIONS OF CANDIDATES
All candidates must meet the following qualifications:
* Must be officially enrolled at IDSC for the First Semester of Academic Year 2026–2027 in any academic program.
* Must be the officially endorsed representative of their respective department for the Search for Mr. and Ms. IDSC 2026.
* Must be a bona fide student of the department they represent.
* Must be of good moral character and possess the poise, confidence, and professionalism expected of an official representative of IDSC.
* Must be in good academic and disciplinary standing, as certified or validated by their respective department.
* Must attend all scheduled meetings, rehearsals, orientations, and official pageant-related activities designated by the Organizing Committee. Failure to attend a required activity without a valid written justification accepted by the Organizing Committee may result in appropriate sanctions, including point deductions, loss of eligibility for applicable special awards, or disqualification, depending on the nature and gravity of the violation.
* Must observe professionalism, respect, sportsmanship, and proper decorum at all times before, during, and after the competition.
* Must not have previously been crowned Mr. IDSC or Ms. IDSC in any prior staging of the pageant.

Only candidates who fully satisfy these qualifications and requirements shall be eligible to compete.

## VII. TABULATION, RANKING, AND FINAL SCORING

### A. Preliminary Round
The Preliminary Round shall consist of:

| Segment | Weight |
| :--- | :--- |
| Production Number | 20% |
| School Uniform | 20% |
| Professional Attire | 20% |
| Modern Barong / Modern Filipiniana | 20% |
| Preliminary Question and Answer | 20% |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** |

Each judge shall independently evaluate every candidate using the prescribed criteria for each segment.

### B. Ranking-Based Scoring per Segment
* The official scoring and placement for every competition segment shall be determined on a ranking basis.
* For each segment, every judge shall first evaluate and assign numerical scores to all candidates based on the prescribed criteria.
* The total numerical score given by each judge shall serve as the basis for that judge’s ranking of the candidates.
* The candidate receiving the highest numerical score from a particular judge shall receive Rank 1, the candidate receiving the next highest score shall receive Rank 2, and so forth.
* The individual rankings given by all members of the Board of Judges shall then be consolidated by the official Tabulation Committee.
* The candidate who obtains the lowest aggregate or total rank shall be considered the highest-ranked candidate for that segment.
* Accordingly, raw numerical scores shall be used to establish each judge’s individual ranking, while the consolidated rankings—not the simple average of the judges’ raw scores—shall determine the official placement of candidates in each segment.

**Tie in an Individual Segment:**
If two (2) or more candidates obtain the same aggregate ranking in an individual competition segment, the candidate with the higher combined raw score from all judges for that particular segment shall receive the higher placement. This provision applies only to the determination of rankings within an individual segment and shall not supersede the separate tie-breaking procedures prescribed for the Top 3, major titles, or special/minor awards.

### C. Determination of the Top 3
* Upon completion of all five (5) Preliminary Round segments, each candidate’s official segment rankings shall be consolidated according to their corresponding weights.
* The three (3) highest-ranked male candidates and three (3) highest-ranked female candidates shall advance to the Final Question and Answer Segment.
* These candidates shall constitute the official Top 3 Finalists for Mr. and Ms. IDSC 2026.

### D. Final Question and Answer Ranking
* The ranking-based scoring system shall likewise apply to the Final Question and Answer Segment.
* Each judge shall independently evaluate and score the Top 3 finalists according to the prescribed Final Q&A criteria.
* The resulting numerical scores shall establish each judge’s individual ranking of the finalists.
* The rankings of all judges shall then be consolidated to determine the official ranking of the finalists in the Final Question and Answer Segment.

### E. Championship Rule and Determination of Winners
For the determination of the final winners, the following weighting shall apply:
* **50%** – Cumulative Preliminary Round Performance
* **50%** – Final Question and Answer Performance

The official Preliminary Round ranking and Final Question and Answer ranking of each finalist shall be consolidated using the prescribed 50%-50% weighting. The finalist who obtains the best final weighted ranking shall be proclaimed Mr. IDSC 2026 or Ms. IDSC 2026, as applicable.

The succeeding finalists shall be proclaimed according to their final weighted rankings:
* Rank 1 – Mr./Ms. IDSC 2026
* Rank 2 – 1st Runner-Up
* Rank 3 – 2nd Runner-Up

### F. TIE-BREAKING PROCEDURE FOR TOP 3 AND MAJOR TITLES
In the event of a tie that directly affects the selection of the Top 3 finalists or the determination of any major title, the tie shall be resolved through a Tie-Breaking Question and Answer Round.
* **Tie Affecting the Top 3:** If two (2) or more candidates are tied for a position that determines qualification to the Top 3, only the candidates involved in the tie shall participate in the tie-breaking round.
* **Tie Affecting the Major Titles:** If two (2) or more finalists are tied in the final ranking for Mr./Ms. IDSC 2026, 1st Runner-Up, or 2nd Runner-Up, only the finalists involved in the tie shall participate in the tie-breaking round.
* To ensure fairness, all candidates involved in the same tie shall answer the same tie-breaking question.
* Each tied candidate shall be given a maximum of sixty (60) seconds to answer.
* Candidates who have not yet answered shall be placed in an appropriate holding area where they cannot hear the question, answer, or response of the candidate currently onstage.
* The Board of Judges shall independently re-evaluate the tied candidates based solely on their responses to the tie-breaking question.

**Tie-Breaking Q&A Criteria:**
| Criterion | Percentage |
| :--- | :--- |
| Content & Substance of Answer | 40% |
| Clarity & Organization of Ideas | 25% |
| Confidence & Delivery | 20% |
| Relevance to the Question | 15% |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** |

* After evaluating the responses, each judge shall rank the tied candidates.
* The rankings of all judges shall be consolidated, and the candidate obtaining the best consolidated ranking shall receive the higher placement.
* The Tie-Breaking Question and Answer Round shall be used solely to resolve the tied position. It shall not alter or replace the candidates’ previously earned scores or rankings.
* If a tie remains after the first tie-breaking round, another question of comparable difficulty shall be asked and the tied candidates shall be rejudged following the same procedure until a clear result is obtained.
* The result of the tie-breaking round, once verified by the Tabulation Committee, shall be final, binding, and non-appealable.

### G. FINALITY AND INTEGRITY OF TABULATION
* All scores and rankings submitted by the Board of Judges shall be considered official upon submission to the Tabulation Committee.
* No submitted score or ranking may be altered after official tabulation has commenced, except for the correction of a duly verified clerical or computational error.
* The official results shall be based solely on the duly accomplished judges’ score sheets and the verified consolidated tabulation.
* The decision of the Board of Judges, as reflected in the verified official tabulation and subject to the prescribed tie-breaking procedures, shall be final, binding, and non-appealable.

### C. TIE-BREAKING PROCEDURE FOR JUDGE-DETERMINED SPECIAL AND MINOR AWARDS
In the event of a tie in any Special or Minor Award determined by the Board of Judges, the following procedure shall apply:
* The Board of Judges shall formally deliberate on the performance of the tied candidates based on the prescribed criteria for the award concerned.
* Following the deliberation, all members of the Board of Judges shall cast a final vote among the candidates involved in the tie.
* The candidate receiving the highest number of votes from the Board of Judges shall be declared the official winner of the award.
* The judges shall base their deliberation and final vote solely on the candidates’ performance, presentation, and compliance with the established criteria for the particular award. Factors unrelated to the prescribed judging criteria shall not be considered.
* If the final vote results in another tie, the Board of Judges shall conduct further deliberation followed by another vote until a clear winner is determined.
* The final decision shall be properly recorded and verified by the Tabulation Committee.
* The decision reached by the Board of Judges through the official deliberation and final voting process shall be final, binding, and non-appealable.

This procedure shall apply to all judge-determined Special and Minor Awards, except awards governed by a separate selection mechanism, including the People’s Choice Award, Mr. and Ms. Congeniality Awards, and Mr. and Ms. IDSC Spirit Awards.

## IX. MECHANICS FOR MR. AND MS. CONGENIALITY AND MR. AND MS. IDSC SPIRIT AWARDS
The Mr. and Ms. Congeniality Awards and Mr. and Ms. IDSC Spirit Awards shall be determined through a secret ballot among the official candidates, separately for the male and female categories.

### A. Candidate Voting
* All official candidates shall be entitled to cast one (1) vote for each applicable award within their respective category.
* Candidates may vote for themselves.
* Voting shall be conducted through secret ballot.

### B. Mr. and Ms. Congeniality
Candidates shall vote for the fellow candidate who, in their judgment, has consistently demonstrated:
* Friendliness;
* Respect;
* Approachability;
* Cooperation;
* Sportsmanship;
* Camaraderie; and
* Positive relationships with fellow candidates throughout the pageant activities.
The candidate receiving the highest number of valid votes shall receive the Mr. or Ms. Congeniality Award.

### C. Mr. and Ms. IDSC Spirit Award
Candidates shall vote for the fellow candidate who, in their judgment, has best demonstrated:
* School pride;
* Enthusiasm;
* Active participation;
* Positive attitude;
* Camaraderie;
* Sportsmanship; and
* The values and spirit of IDSC throughout the competition and related activities.
The candidate receiving the highest number of valid votes shall receive the Mr. or Ms. IDSC Spirit Award.

### D. Tie-Breaking Procedure
In the event of a tie for the highest number of votes:
* A runoff vote shall be conducted.
* Only the names of the candidates involved in the tie shall appear on the runoff ballot.
* All other candidates within the same category shall vote again.
* Candidates involved in the tie shall be excluded from participating in the runoff vote.
* Each eligible candidate shall cast one (1) vote for one of the tied candidates.
* The tied candidate receiving the highest number of votes in the runoff shall be declared the winner.
* Should another tie occur, another runoff vote shall be conducted following the same procedure until a clear winner is determined.
* All ballots shall be counted and verified by the Organizing Committee. Individual voting choices shall remain confidential, and only the official results shall be announced.
* The verified results shall be final and non-appealable.

## X. MECHANICS FOR THE PEOPLE’S CHOICE AWARD
* The People’s Choice Award shall be determined through legitimate Facebook reactions to the candidates’ official individual photos.
* Official candidate photos shall be posted on September 6, 2026, at 5:00 PM on the official Facebook page designated by the Organizing Committee.

* Only reactions recorded within the official voting period shall be counted. Reactions received after the deadline shall not be included in the final count.
* The male and female candidates receiving the highest total number of verified and legitimate reactions shall receive the respective People’s Choice Awards.
* The Organizing Committee reserves the right to audit, verify, and invalidate suspicious or illegitimate engagements, including those reasonably determined to have resulted from fake accounts, automated tools, artificial boosting, or paid engagement services.
* Any verified attempt to deliberately manipulate the voting process may result in disqualification from the People’s Choice Award and the imposition of appropriate sanctions.
* The verified results of the People’s Choice Award shall be final and non-appealable.

To maintain fairness, security, order, and professionalism:
* Outside hair and makeup artists, stylists, or personal assistants shall not be permitted backstage during the pageant proper.
* Each candidate may have a maximum of two (2) department-appointed assistants backstage.
* All authorized assistants must be officially endorsed by the candidate’s department and recognized by the Organizing Committee.
* Candidates are strongly encouraged to have their primary hair and makeup completed before 7:00 AM on pageant day. This may be done professionally outside the pageant venue or through arrangements made by the candidate’s department.
* During the pageant proper, only authorized candidates, organizers, production personnel, marshals, and officially designated department assistants shall be permitted backstage.
* Only light hair and makeup retouching shall be permitted backstage.
* Unauthorized individuals found backstage shall be required to leave immediately.
* Violations of the backstage policy may result in appropriate sanctions, including point deductions or disqualification, depending on the severity and circumstances of the violation.

## XII. GENERAL RULES AND CONDUCT
All candidates, departments, assistants, and supporters must strictly observe the following:

* **Opening Parade and Punctuality.** All candidates must participate in the Opening Parade wearing a dark green top and white bottoms and carrying or accompanying their respective department banner. Formation shall be held inside the school campus at the designated assembly area before proceeding to the venue. * **Professional Conduct.** Candidates are expected to demonstrate professionalism, courtesy, respect, sportsmanship, and proper decorum throughout all pageant-related activities.
* **Prohibited Conduct.** Unsportsmanlike behavior, harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, deliberate disruption, or other serious misconduct committed onstage, backstage, during rehearsals, at official activities, or through online platforms may result in appropriate disciplinary action.

* **Props, Music, and Special Effects.** All props, music, technical requirements, and special effects requiring prior clearance must be submitted to and approved by the Organizing Committee before use.
* **Backstage Access.** Backstage access shall be strictly controlled. Only duly authorized individuals shall be permitted within restricted areas.

* **Integrity of the Competition.** Any verified attempt to improperly influence a judge, manipulate official scores or voting results, sabotage another candidate, tamper with tabulation, or otherwise compromise the integrity of the competition may constitute grounds for disqualification and further disciplinary action.
* **Penalties and Due Process.** Where appropriate, penalties shall be imposed only after the circumstances of the alleged violation have been reasonably verified by the Organizing Committee and/or appropriate school authorities.
* **Amendments.** The Organizing Committee reserves the right to issue reasonable clarifications, amendments, or supplementary rules when necessary to preserve fairness, safety, order, and the integrity of the competition. Any material change affecting judging, scoring, eligibility, or candidate obligations shall be formally communicated to all participating departments and candidates.
* **Acceptance of Rules.** Participation in the Search for Mr. and Ms. IDSC 2026 constitutes acknowledgment and acceptance of these Official Pageant Mechanics and Guidelines.

## XIII. POST-CORONATION RESPONSIBILITIES, ACCOUNTABILITY, DUE PROCESS, AND SUCCESSION

### A. ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITY OF THE TITLEHOLDERS
* The proclaimed Mr. IDSC 2026 and Ms. IDSC 2026 shall serve as official student ambassadors of the institution throughout their reign.
* The titles carry not only recognition and distinction but also the responsibility to demonstrate leadership, service, integrity, social responsibility, and active participation in the school community.
* The titleholders are expected to uphold the values and good reputation of IDSC and to serve as positive representatives of the institution during their reign.

### B. MANDATORY ADVOCACY PROJECT
As part of their official responsibilities, Mr. IDSC 2026 and Ms. IDSC 2026 shall each conceptualize, develop, and implement at least one (1) advocacy-based project before the completion of their reign.

The advocacy project shall:
* Be substantially aligned with the advocacy presented or represented by the titleholder during the Search for Mr. and Ms. IDSC 2026;
* Address a meaningful need, concern, or cause benefiting the IDSC community and/or the wider community;
* Be planned and implemented in collaboration with the College Student Council;
* Be appropriately coordinated with the Office of Student Affairs and other concerned school offices, organizations, or community partners, when necessary;
* Be realistic, achievable, inclusive, responsible, and consistent with the policies, values, and available resources of IDSC;
* Receive all necessary institutional approvals before implementation; and
* Be fully implemented before the official conclusion of the titleholder’s reign or before the coronation of their successor, whichever comes first.

Mr. IDSC 2026 and Ms. IDSC 2026 may collaborate on a common advocacy initiative; however, the project must clearly demonstrate the meaningful and identifiable participation and contribution of each titleholder toward the fulfillment of their respective advocacy responsibilities. The titleholders shall coordinate with the College Student Council regarding the planning, scheduling, approval, implementation, promotion, and proper documentation of their respective advocacy projects.
The completed advocacy project shall form part of the titleholder’s official legacy and shall demonstrate that the title of Mr. or Ms. IDSC represents not only distinction but also leadership translated into meaningful action and service. Failure to fulfill this responsibility without a valid and justifiable reason shall be referred to the appropriate school authorities for review and appropriate action in accordance with existing institutional policies and due process.

### C. GROUNDS FOR DISQUALIFICATION OR DETHRONEMENT
The proclaimed Mr. IDSC 2026 or Ms. IDSC 2026 may be subject to disqualification or dethronement after coronation if, following proper investigation and due process, the titleholder is found responsible for:
* Serious misconduct;
* Material violation of school policies;
* Conduct that substantially compromises the integrity and dignity of the title;
* Serious acts that demonstrably damage the reputation of IDSC or the Search for Mr. and Ms. IDSC; or
* Other serious violations warranting removal under applicable institutional policies.

Dethronement shall not be imposed solely on the basis of allegations, rumors, unverified social media content, or personal disputes.

### D. DUE PROCESS AND FORMAL INVESTIGATION
* Any allegation that may warrant post-coronation disciplinary action shall not result in automatic dethronement.
* The matter shall be subject to appropriate fact-finding, formal review, and due process in accordance with applicable IDSC policies and procedures.
* The concerned titleholder shall be:
  * Properly informed of the allegation or complaint;
  * Given a reasonable opportunity to respond;
  * Permitted to present relevant evidence or explanation; and
  * Accorded the applicable rights and procedures provided under existing institutional policies.

### E. DECISION AND IMPLEMENTATION
Any final decision concerning disqualification or dethronement shall be made by the appropriate school authority or authorities in accordance with institutional policies and procedures, with the participation or coordination of the concerned offices as may be appropriate, including the:
* Office of the President;
* Office of the Vice President for Administration;
* Office of the Vice President for Academic Affairs;
* Office of Student Affairs; and
* Organizing Committee.

Any decision shall become official and enforceable only upon proper issuance by the duly authorized school authority.

### F. SUCCESSION RULE
* Should the reigning Mr. IDSC 2026 or Ms. IDSC 2026 be officially dethroned, become permanently unable to fulfill the responsibilities of the title, formally relinquish the title, or otherwise be removed in accordance with applicable institutional rules, the 1st Runner-Up of the same category shall succeed to the title for the remainder of the term.
* The succeeding titleholder shall assume the official responsibilities, duties, rights, and privileges attached to the title, including any outstanding advocacy and institutional responsibilities applicable to the position.
* If succession by the 1st Runner-Up is not possible, the appropriate school authorities, in consultation with the Organizing Committee, shall determine the proper succession based on the official final results and applicable institutional policies.

### G. PRESERVATION OF SPECIAL AWARDS
* Unless otherwise expressly stated in an official decision, Special or Minor Awards previously received by a candidate shall remain valid.
* A Special or Minor Award may be withdrawn only when the established violation directly affects the candidate’s eligibility for, legitimacy of, or basis upon which that particular award was granted.

* These guidelines are established to ensure a competition founded on fairness, transparency, integrity, professionalism, sportsmanship, accountability, and respect, consistent with the significance of the IDSC 18th Founding Anniversary Celebration.
