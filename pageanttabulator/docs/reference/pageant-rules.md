## I. General Description

Mr. and Ms. IDSC 2026 is the official search for the male and female students who best embody the intelligence, character, leadership, and pride of IDSC, held as part of the grand celebration of the **18th Founding Anniversary** of the institution.

This pageant is not merely a display of beauty, but a holistic celebration of personality, intelligence, character, talent, and advocacy. It aims to promote camaraderie, excellence, and healthy competition among all departments of IDSC as the institution marks eighteen (18) remarkable years of service and excellence in education.

The competition serves as a platform for recognition and representation, honoring candidates who embody grace, intelligence, leadership, confidence, and pride while representing their respective departments with excellence and integrity.

---

## II. Representation and Candidacy

- Each department must officially send **TWO (2) representatives**: one (1) male and one (1) female — who will compete separately as individual candidates for Mr. and Ms. IDSC 2026.
- Each candidate shall represent their respective department throughout the competition.
- Official names of the two representatives must be submitted **on or before August 26, 2026** to the organizing committee.
- Only candidates officially endorsed by their department and validated by the organizers shall be allowed to compete.

---

## III. Qualifications of Candidates

All candidates must meet the following requirements:

1. Must be officially enrolled for the First Semester of Academic Year 2026–2027 at IDSC in any academic program.
2. Must be an officially registered representative of their department for the IDSC 18th Founding Anniversary Celebration.
3. Must be a bona fide student of the department they are representing, of good moral character, and possesses the poise and confidence to represent IDSC with pride and dignity.
4. Must be in good academic and disciplinary standing as certified by their respective department.
5. Must attend all scheduled rehearsals, meetings, and official activities set by the organizers.
6. Failure to attend rehearsals without a valid written justification approved by the organizing committee may result in point deductions, loss of eligibility for special awards, or disqualification.
7. Must conduct themselves with professionalism, respect, and good decorum at all times before, during, and after the pageant.
8. Must **not** have been previously crowned Mr. or Ms. IDSC in any prior staging of the pageant.

Only candidates who fully comply with these qualifications shall be allowed to compete.

## VI. Question and Answer (Q&A) Segment

### A. Preliminary Q&A (For Top 5 Selection)

- All candidates will receive preliminary questions on September 9, 2026.
- On pageant day, the final preliminary question will be chosen via draw lots.
- Each candidate will have **60 seconds** to answer.
- Language options: Pure English or Pure Filipino (Tagalog) only.
- From this round, the **Top 5 male candidates** and **Top 5 female candidates** will be selected.

**Criteria:**

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Content & Substance of Answer | 40% |
| Clarity & Organization of Ideas | 25% |
| Confidence & Delivery | 20% |
| Relevance to the Question | 15% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

### B. Final Q&A (For Top 3 Selection)

- The Top 5 (per category) will answer a new question selected via draw lots on pageant day.
- Each finalist will have **60 seconds** to answer.

**Judging Criteria for Q&A:**

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Content & Substance of Answer | 40% |
| Clarity & Organization of Ideas | 25% |
| Confidence & Delivery | 20% |
| Relevance to the Question | 15% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

**From this round, winners will be determined per category:**
- Mr. / Ms. IDSC 2026 (Champion)
- 1st Runner-Up
- 2nd Runner-Up

## VII. Tabulation of Scores

### Top 5 Selection Rule

The candidates with the **top 5 highest preliminary round cumulative score**, per category (male and female), shall become part of the final roster to vie for Mr. and Ms. IDSC 2026.

### Championship Rule

The Final Question and Answer segment shall account for **50% of the final overall score**, while the candidate's Preliminary Round cumulative score shall account for the remaining **50%**.

### Final Score Computation

```
Final Score = (Preliminary Round Total Score × 50%)
            + (Final Q&A Score × 50%)
```

The combined score from these two components shall determine the winners.

## VIII. Special Awards

The following special awards shall be given **per category (male and female)**, unless otherwise stated:

1. Best in Production Number
2. Best in School Uniform
3. Best in Professional Attire
4. Best in Modern Barong / Best in Filipiniana
5. Best in Advocacy
6. Mr./Ms. IDSC Spirit Award
7. Mr./Ms. Photogenic
8. People's Choice Award
9. Mr./Ms. Congeniality
10. Best in Ramp

### Mechanics for People's Choice Award

The People's Choice Award shall be determined through Facebook reactions:

- Official individual photos of all candidates will be posted on the official IDSC Facebook Page.
- Candidates and their departments are allowed to share their official photo and invite supporters to react.
- Valid reactions include: Like, Love, Care, Wow, Haha, and other standard Facebook reactions. **All reactions count equally.**
- Voting period shall officially end **forty-eight (48) hours** after the posting of official candidate photos.
- The candidate whose photo obtains the highest total number of legitimate reactions within 48 hours shall be declared winner.
- The organizing committee reserves the right to audit, verify, and invalidate suspicious reactions.
- Any attempt to manipulate votes may result in disqualification from the People's Choice Award and possible sanctions.
- The results of the People's Choice Award are **final and non-appealable**.

## IX. Hair, Makeup, and Backstage Policy

- Hiring of outside hair and makeup artists, stylists, or assistants is **NOT allowed** backstage.
- Each candidate may only have a maximum of **two (2) department-appointed assistants** backstage.
- Candidates are encouraged to have hair and makeup done before **1:00 PM** on pageant day.
- During the pageant proper, no outsiders are allowed backstage.
- Only light retouching is permitted backstage.
- Violation of this rule may result in point deductions or disqualification.

## X. General Rules and Conduct

1. Judges' decisions are final, binding, and irrevocable. No appeals shall be entertained after results are announced.
2. **Opening Parade and Punctuality Rule.** All candidates must wear their official department shirt and hold their department banner during the Opening Parade. Candidates must report at least **thirty (30) minutes** before the scheduled formation time. Late arrival or absence without approved justification may result in point deductions or disqualification.
3. Any form of unsportsmanlike behavior, disrespect, harassment, discrimination, bullying, or misconduct shall be grounds for point deductions or immediate disqualification.
4. Posting negative, defamatory, or offensive comments about other candidates, judges, or organizers on social media is strictly prohibited.
5. All props, music, and special effects must be submitted for approval before use.
6. Candidates must comply with all backstage policies. Unauthorized individuals will be escorted out immediately.
7. Departments are responsible for the behavior of their supporters.
8. Any attempt to manipulate scores, sabotage another candidate, or influence judges is strictly prohibited and punishable by disqualification.
9. The organizing committee reserves the right to revise, amend, or update these mechanics whenever necessary.
10. By joining, all candidates and departments agree to abide by these rules in full.

## XI. Post-Coronation Accountability, Due Process, and Succession

### Grounds for Disqualification or Dethronement

The proclaimed Mr. or Ms. IDSC 2026 may be disqualified or dethroned even after coronation if it is established that they have been involved in any form of misconduct, misbehavior, violation of school policies, or actions that tarnish the reputation of IDSC.

### Due Process and Formal Investigation

- Any allegation of misconduct shall not result in automatic dethronement.
- The matter shall be subjected to a formal complaint, fact-finding process, and due process in accordance with IDSC's existing disciplinary policies.
- The candidate shall be given the right to be heard, to respond to allegations, and to present evidence in their defense.

### Decision and Implementation

The final decision shall be made by the appropriate school authorities in coordination with:
- Office of the President
- Office of the Vice President for Administration
- Office of the Vice President for Academic Affairs
- Office of Student Affairs
- The organizing committee

### Succession Rule

In the event that the crowned Mr. or Ms. IDSC 2026 is officially dethroned, the **1st Runner-Up (of the same category)** shall automatically assume the title for the remaining term.

### Preservation of Special Awards

Unless otherwise stated in the final decision, previously received special awards shall remain valid unless directly related to the misconduct.
